What it does

The front desk work that shouldn't need a person

Answers from your policies

Timings, doctors' days, services, location, parking, payment and cancellation rules. Every answer comes from a knowledge base you approve.

Books appointments

Checks free slots in your sheet or CRM, books the patient, and sends a WhatsApp or SMS confirmation and a reminder the day before.

Reschedules and cancels

Moves or cancels existing bookings by phone or WhatsApp and frees the slot for someone else.

Hands off cleanly

Anything outside its knowledge, anything clinical, or "let me talk to a person" goes to your team with a one-line summary.

Logs every conversation

Caller, reason, outcome and a short summary go into the sheet, so nothing depends on someone remembering.

Shows it all locally

The dashboard app on your front-desk computer shows today's bookings, handoffs and missed calls in real time.

Setup

Live in about two weeks

  1. Policy interview

    A 45-minute call with you and your front desk to capture how you actually run: hours, services, rules and exceptions.

  2. Knowledge base and booking sheet

    We turn that into an approved knowledge base and set up your booking sheet with slots, chairs and doctors, in your own Google account.

  3. Test and sign-off

    You and your staff call it, try to trip it up, and approve the answers. Languages are tested with your team.

  4. Go live, gradually

    Start with after-hours and overflow calls, then widen once you're comfortable. Your existing number stays the same.

  5. Weekly tune-up

    We review handoffs and unanswered questions every week and add what's missing, with your approval.

FAQ

About the AI Receptionist

What happens when a patient asks something the receptionist doesn't know?

It says it will get the team to help. During clinic hours it transfers the call to a staff member with a one-line summary. After hours it logs a callback request in your sheet and alerts your team on WhatsApp. It never guesses.

Will it give medical advice or quote treatment prices?

No medical advice, ever. It quotes only prices you choose to publish, such as a consultation fee. Everything else is routed to your team.

How does it know our clinic's policies?

We build a private knowledge base from documents you approve: services, timings, doctors' days, location and parking, payment and insurance policy, cancellation rules, and pre-visit instructions. You review and sign off on it before go-live, and changes take effect the same day.

Which languages does it speak?

English and Hindi are well supported by today's voice platforms. For Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Arabic and others, we test quality with your own staff before go-live and only switch on languages that pass.

Where do bookings go?

Into a Google Sheet in your clinic's Google account, which acts as your appointment book, or into your CRM where it has an API. If your appointments live in practice software without an API, bookings land in the sheet as confirmed slots for your front desk to copy across.

What is the local dashboard app?

A small web app installed on your front-desk computer. It signs in with your clinic's Google account and reads the sheet directly, showing today's bookings, calls handled, handoffs and missed calls. Patient data does not pass through Advaya's servers.

Can patients tell it's an AI?

Yes. It introduces itself as the clinic's virtual assistant. Patients can say 'talk to a person' at any time and are transferred.
