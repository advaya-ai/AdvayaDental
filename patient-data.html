Patient data & compliance

How we handle your patients' data

Short version: we don't build our own systems to hold patient records. Every step runs on an established platform that signs the agreement healthcare requires. Bookings live in a Google Sheet that you own.

Last reviewed: 7 November 2026. Vendor terms change, so we re-check them before every new clinic goes live.

No tool makes a clinic "compliant" by itself. HIPAA and India's DPDP Act are about how an organisation behaves, not which software it buys. The platforms below carry the infrastructure side, we carry our part under a written agreement, and some duties always stay with the clinic.

United States: HIPAA

For US practices we act as a Business Associate and sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with you before any patient data is shared. Every platform in the chain also signs a BAA.

What we deliberately don't use for US patient data

  • WhatsApp. Meta doesn't sign BAAs, so WhatsApp is off the table for US patients.
  • General-purpose automation tools without a BAA (for example, Make or n8n Cloud). We use them only for non-patient work.
  • Personal Gmail or free Google accounts. The booking sheet must sit in a paid Workspace with the BAA accepted.

India: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

Your clinic is the Data Fiduciary; we are a Data Processor acting on your instructions under a written data processing agreement. The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025 and come into force in phases, with most obligations expected from May 2027. We're building to them now.

The local dashboard app

The front-desk dashboard runs on your clinic's computer and signs in with your clinic's Google account. It reads the booking sheet directly from Google, so patient data doesn't pass through Advaya's servers. Because it runs on your machine, keep that computer locked when unattended and use your operating system's disk encryption.
