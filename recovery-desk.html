Where the money goes

The quietest leak is the list you already have

Unscheduled treatment

Plans that were presented, maybe even accepted, but never scheduled. Your software already has this report.

Overdue recall

Patients 6 to 18 months past their last check-up. Each one who returns often brings newly diagnosed work.

Empty cancellation slots

When a slot opens at short notice, we offer it first to high-value patients on your waitlist.

We don't quote industry averages at you. Most published dental "revenue leak" figures come from companies selling the fix. Your own reports give your real number, and that's what the free audit measures.

How it works

Six steps, set up in about two weeks

  1. Free audit

    You share three standard reports: unscheduled treatment, overdue recall and recent cancellations. We put a figure on what's there.

  2. Rules you approve

    Who to contact, which slots to offer, the doctor's name we use, and the exact wording.

  3. Nightly list

    The list refreshes every night, so patients who book on their own drop off automatically.

  4. Friendly outreach

    A short WhatsApp or SMS with a one-tap booking link. No diagnosis, no prices, no pressure.

  5. Callback for the quiet ones

    One AI voice callback that only books or passes the patient to your team. Pain or an emergency goes straight to a human.

  6. Monday report

    Contacts, replies, bookings, attendance and booked production, line by line.

Fit

Is the Recovery Desk right for your clinic?

A good fit if

  • You have been open two or more years and have a real patient base.
  • Your software can export an unscheduled-treatment or overdue-recall report.
  • Your front desk never has time to chase old treatment plans.

Probably not a fit if

  • Your list is small. We will tell you on the free audit that it isn't worth paying for.
  • You are already booked solid for weeks.
  • You want messages that mention diagnoses or prices. Ours never do.
FAQ

About the Recovery Desk

Is the Recovery Desk an AI receptionist?

No. It doesn't touch your incoming calls. It only follows up with your existing patients who have a treatment plan or a check-up due, and hands warm replies to your team.

Which practice software does it work with?

In the US we connect through NexHealth Synchronizer, which supports Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Curve Hero and others. Elsewhere we start from a scheduled report export, so any system that can export a patient list works.

Do messages mention the patient's treatment?

No. Messages say the doctor asked us to follow up and offer a booking link. Diagnoses, procedure names and prices never appear in a message.

How is recovered production measured?

Every booking is tied to a named patient on your original list. The Monday report shows appointments booked and attended, and their value from your own fee schedule.

What if my list is small?

Then we'll tell you on the free audit that it isn't worth paying for.

How much does the Recovery Desk cost?

In India it is ₹9,999 a month, or ₹14,999 for clinics with 3 or more chairs, with a ₹15,000 setup that is waived after the pilot. In the US it is $750 a month with a $1,000 setup, waived after a 30-day pilot. Prices exclude GST and messages are billed at cost.

How long does setup take?

About two weeks, starting from three standard reports: unscheduled treatment, overdue recall and recent cancellations.
