Where the money actually goes

Missed calls get all the attention. The bigger leak is usually quieter: patients who already trust you, already have a plan, and simply never came back. Nobody on a busy front desk has time to chase them.

How it works

Six steps, set up in about two weeks

  1. Free audit

    You share three standard reports: unscheduled treatment, overdue recall, and recent cancellations. We put a rupee or dollar figure on what's sitting there and tell you honestly whether it's worth working.

  2. Rules you approve

    You choose which patients to contact, which slots we may offer, the doctor's name we use, and the exact message wording. Nothing goes out that you haven't seen.

  3. Nightly list

    Each night the list refreshes from your practice software, so patients who booked on their own or were seen today drop off automatically.

  4. Friendly outreach

    A short WhatsApp or SMS message from your clinic with a one-tap booking link. No diagnosis, no prices, no pressure in the message itself.

  5. Callback for the quiet ones

    Patients who don't reply after a few days get one AI voice callback whose only job is to book or pass them to your team. Any mention of pain or an emergency goes straight to a human.

  6. Monday report

    Contacts, replies, bookings, attendance and booked production, line by line, so you can check every booking in your own software.

FAQ

Questions clinics ask us

Is this an AI receptionist?

No. Your phones stay exactly as they are. The Recovery Desk only does outbound follow-up with patients who already have a treatment plan or are overdue for recall, and hands warm replies to your team.

Which practice software do you work with?

In the US we connect through NexHealth Synchronizer, which supports Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Curve Hero, Denticon and others. In India and elsewhere we start from a scheduled report export from your clinic software, so any system that can export a patient list works.

Do you send treatment details over WhatsApp?

No. Messages say the doctor asked us to follow up and offer a booking link. Diagnosis, procedure names and prices are never put in a message. In the US we don't use WhatsApp for patients at all; we use SMS and voice through vendors that sign Business Associate Agreements.

How is recovered production measured?

Every booking is tied to a named patient on your original list. The weekly report shows appointments booked, attended, and the production value from your own fee schedule, so you can check every line in your software.

What does the guarantee cover?

If booked recovered production in a month is less than five times that month's fee, the next month is free. The target and how it's measured are written down before the pilot starts.

What if my list is small?

Then we'll tell you on the free audit that it isn't worth paying for. New practices and clinics with a strong treatment coordinator often have little to recover.

Will patients be annoyed?

We cap it at three touches per patient per campaign, use your clinic's name and tone, and stop immediately on any opt-out. Messages are short and helpful, not sales copy.
