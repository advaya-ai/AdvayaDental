Patient data & compliance

How we handle your patients' data

Short version: we don't build our own systems to hold patient records. Every step runs on an established platform that signs the agreement healthcare requires, and we keep messages free of clinical detail.

Last reviewed: 7 November 2026. Vendor terms change; we re-check them before every new clinic goes live.

No tool makes a clinic "compliant" by itself. HIPAA and India's DPDP Act are about how an organisation behaves, not about which software it buys. The platforms below carry the infrastructure side. We carry our part under a written agreement. Some duties always stay with the clinic. The table further down spells out who does what.

United States: HIPAA

For US practices we act as a Business Associate and sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with you before any patient data is shared. Every platform in the chain also signs a BAA.

What we deliberately don't use for US patient data

  • WhatsApp. Meta's terms say it isn't a Business Associate and won't sign a BAA, so WhatsApp is off the table for US patients.
  • General-purpose automation tools without a BAA (for example, Make or n8n Cloud). We use them only for non-patient work, such as our own sales follow-up.
  • Email for patient lists. Lists move between the platforms above, never as attachments.

Account ownership

Where possible, the platform accounts are opened in your practice's name, with your practice signing each vendor's BAA directly, and our team added as users. If you ever stop working with us, you keep the accounts, the data and the audit trail, and we remove our access the same day.

India: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

Under the DPDP Act your clinic is the Data Fiduciary and we are a Data Processor acting on your instructions under a written data processing agreement. The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025 and come into force in phases, with most obligations expected to apply from May 2027. We're building to those obligations now.
